Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download and customize these invitation templates on Creative Fabrica?

After clicking any invitation link, you will be redirected to Creative Fabrica where you can instantly download the files. Most suites include editable Canva links, Photoshop PSD files, or editable PDFs. Open the file in your preferred software, swap the placeholder text with your wedding details, and adjust fonts or colors as desired.

Can I print these at home or with a professional print shop?

Yes, all templates are created in high-resolution (300 DPI) industry-standard print formats. You can print them on your home cardstock printer or export high-quality PDFs with bleed lines for professional local or online printers (such as Cards & Pockets, Smartpress, or local boutique print shops).

Are matching RSVP cards, details cards, and fonts included?

Most complete bundles listed include matching RSVP cards, reception details cards, and save-the-date templates. The description page for each product lists font links or includes embedded fonts, ensuring your entire suite maintains a cohesive aesthetic.
